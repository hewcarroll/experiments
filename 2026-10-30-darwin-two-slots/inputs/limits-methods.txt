One card, one model, one cooling setup. Settings were compared in cool-started bursts, which compares settings, not sustained speed. Some settings were screened on the 3B model first. Community numbers cited in the write-up were not rerun here. Since driver 580.65.06, nvidia-smi reports clocks from before thermal slowdowns are applied, so throttling was read from the clock-event reason bits and the measured token rates rather than the clock column alone.
